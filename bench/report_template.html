Fiber-seq · single-molecule chromatin · simulation benchmark

fiberstate: sequence-aware nucleosome and footprint calling

A new caller that labels every base of every Fiber-seq molecule as linker, nucleosome or TF footprint, benchmarked against fibertools-rs v0.13.1 and two classic baselines on simulated molecules with known ground truth, then compared with fibertools, FiberHMM 3.0.2 and HiddenFoot on real GM12878 and K562 Fiber-seq data at CTCF and REST sites labelled by ChIP-seq.

Simulation headline (m6A sensitivity 0.6, false-positive rate 0.02); see the real-data section for how these held up

What the tool does differently

  • G/C bases are treated as no evidence. Hia5 only methylates A, so a GC-rich stretch is m6A-free whether or not it is wrapped. Gap-based callers read that silence as protection.
  • Methylation rate is learned per molecule. A short EM re-estimates the accessible and protected m6A rates for each fiber, so poorly methylated fibers don't create long fake nucleosomes.
  • Nucleosomes and footprints are decoded jointly with an explicit-duration hidden semi-Markov model (~147 bp nucleosomes plus hexasomes, 8–90 bp footprints), and each call carries a posterior confidence.
  • Co-occupancy is conditioned on accessibility. Two sites inside the same open element look correlated just because they open together; fiberstate down-weights molecules where either site is nucleosome-bound.

Detecting cooperative TF binding

TF binding at ChIP-seq-labelled motif sites

Strong motif matches on chr19 (CTCF) or eight chromosomes (REST, NRF1), labelled bound (within 100 bp of an ENCODE IDR peak summit) or unbound (no peak within 2 kb), with unbound sites matched to the bound ones on motif score. Each tool's per-site share of footprinted molecules is scored by AUROC; p-values in brackets are paired DeLong tests of fiberstate against each tool. "Weak" and "strong ChIP" are the lowest and highest thirds of bound sites by ChIP signal, each scored against all unbound sites. fiberstate's footprint threshold was chosen on the CTCF sites; REST and NRF1 were run afterwards with no retuning. fibertools is told the motif positions; HiddenFoot is given the motif matrix.

At a matched false-positive rate

Site AUROC only ranks sites; it does not reward a caller for making fewer false calls. Here each tool's per-molecule score (fiberstate: mean footprint posterior over the motif; FiberHMM: quality of a footprint covering the motif; HiddenFoot: motif posterior; fibertools: its yes/no call) is cut so that exactly 1% of molecules at unbound sites are called footprinted, and the table shows the share of molecules at bound sites called at that cut. Tied scores and yes/no calls are read off each tool's ROC curve. Brackets: fiberstate minus that tool, in percentage points, with a site bootstrap p-value.

Real data: GM12878 Fiber-seq

No ground truth exists on real data, so these checks are indirect. GM12878 chr19:45.0–45.5 Mb: 3,125 molecules (median 16.4 kb, ~97× coverage) streamed from the public FIRE data, compared with the 255 FIRE accessibility peaks for that region. Accessibility is also scored against ENCODE DNase-seq peaks.

Enzyme sequence preference. In confidently open DNA, m6A calls at A-tracts (AAAAA, TTAAT, TAAAA) are 15–25× rarer than average, and A's flanked by G/C are marked about 1.4× more. Tables learned separately on the two datasets agree closely (r = 0.987 on log weights). fiberstate now uses this table by default.
